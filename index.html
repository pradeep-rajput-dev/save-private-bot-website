Help center

Frequently asked questions.

Tap any question to expand the answer. The + icon indicates a closed item.

How do I start using the bot?

Open the Telegram bot, press Start or use /start, then follow the instructions shown by the bot.

What type of Telegram content can I process?

The bot supports the content types and workflows exposed inside the bot, including supported videos, PDFs, photos, audio and text.

Does the bot support large files?

The service is designed for large-file workflows. Actual limits can depend on Telegram, the selected plan, file type and the bot's current technical limits.

Can I use batch or range mode?

Batch and range features are available according to the active subscription plan and the commands/features currently enabled in the bot.

What is the daily usage limit?

Your daily limit depends on your active plan. The Free plan currently shows 2 uses/day, Basic 10, Standard 50, Premium 100 and Ultimate unlimited daily usage.

When does my subscription start?

Subscription timing and activation are determined by the successful payment and activation process used by the bot. Check /myplan after activation.

Can I buy another plan while I already have one?

Yes. The bot supports plan handling for an existing subscription. Depending on the purchased tier, a plan may extend the current plan, activate as an upgrade, or be queued for later.

Will a lower plan replace my current higher plan?

A lower-tier purchase is handled according to the bot's subscription logic and can be queued rather than immediately replacing an active higher-tier plan.

Can I upgrade while a lower plan is active?

A higher-tier purchase can be activated as an upgrade according to the bot's current subscription logic.

Where can I check my plan?

Use the bot's /myplan command where available to view your current subscription information.

How do I get support?

Use the support contact listed on this website. Include your Telegram user ID and relevant order/payment reference when contacting support about an account or payment.

What if a payment is deducted but my plan is not activated?

Contact support with your Telegram user ID, payment/order reference and transaction details. Do not submit the same payment repeatedly while the original transaction is being checked.

Can I share my subscription with another Telegram account?

Subscription access is associated with the Telegram account handled by the bot. Any transfer feature is subject to the bot's currently available transfer rules.

Can I use custom thumbnails?

Custom thumbnail features are available where supported by the active plan and bot commands.

Can I control watermarks?

Watermark controls vary by plan. The plan section on this website describes the current advertised watermark options.

Is the service guaranteed to be available 24/7?

The service is intended to operate continuously, but temporary interruptions can occur because of Telegram, hosting, maintenance, network, software or other technical issues.

Can a download fail even if my subscription is active?

Yes. A valid subscription does not guarantee that every individual source, Telegram message, URL or file will always be retrievable. Source availability, permissions, Telegram restrictions and technical conditions can affect a request.

Does the bot guarantee a particular download speed?

No fixed per-user speed is guaranteed. Speed can vary with Telegram, source servers, network routing, server load, file size and other technical conditions.

Can the bot's features or limits change?

Features, supported sources, limits and technical implementation may be changed, improved, restricted or temporarily disabled when necessary for service operation.

Where can I see the current prices?

The current advertised prices are shown in the Plans section of this website and inside the bot. If there is a difference caused by a temporary offer or bot update, follow the price shown at the point of purchase.
